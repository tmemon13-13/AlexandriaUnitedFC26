About the Club

Our Story

A community soccer club representing Alexandria and the greater DMV, built on pride, passion, and a relentless love for the beautiful game.

Who We Are

Rooted in Alexandria

Alexandria United FC is a community-driven soccer club competing in the UPSL Premier, representing Alexandria, Virginia and the broader Northern Virginia region on the pitch.

We're more than a scoreline. The club exists to give local players a competitive, professional-caliber platform to grow, and to give the Alexandria community a team worth rallying behind, match after match, season after season.

From the coaching staff to the front office, everyone involved shares the same goal: build something the city can be proud of, on and off the field.

Our Mission

Football for the whole city, played with pride, built on community.

We're committed to competing at the highest level of the UPSL Premier while creating real opportunity for local players, coaches, and businesses to grow alongside the club.
