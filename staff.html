Ownership
Hatzael Diaz

Hatzael Diaz is the founder and owner of Alexandria United FC. Driven by a passion for soccer and a belief in the power of community, Hatzael built AUFC from the ground up, creating a club that reflects the spirit and diversity of Alexandria, Virginia. His vision continues to shape every aspect of the organization, from the pitch to the front office.
